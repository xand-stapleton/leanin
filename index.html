Lean In:Proofs to Learn Lean

A small, concrete set of formal proofs with Lean and mathlib: each designed to introduce slightly more of Lean and the libraries needed to do useful things with it!

The route

    A note on ordering

    The opening proofs introduce automation, hypotheses, extensionality, and witnesses. The middle of the collection slowly adds library lemmas, finite sets, induction, abstract algebra, and finite types. The later proofs are actually useful results for, matrices, polynomials, irrationality, graph theory, and (mostly complex) analysis.
